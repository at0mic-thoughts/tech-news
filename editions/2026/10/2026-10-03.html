⚖️ Court Agrees with EFF: Utah's VPN Law Is Technically Impossible

A federal court ruled that Utah's law requiring VPN providers to age-verify and block content demands something that is technically impossible to implement, handing the EFF a clean and significant win. This matters because legislators keep writing internet laws that assume ISPs and VPN providers have magical powers they simply do not have.

🎲 AI Finally Beats the Best Stratego Player in History

An AI system cracked Stratego, the hidden-information board game that resisted AI solutions for decades, and apparently did it on a relatively modest budget. Imperfect-information games are a fundamentally harder problem class than chess or Go, so this is a genuine research milestone worth tracking.

💸 2 to 6 Percent of World Bank Aid Siphoned into Crypto Wallets

An NBER paper finds a meaningful slice of World Bank foreign aid ended up in crypto wallets, which is alarming as a governance story and fascinating as real-world evidence of how crypto flows in high-corruption contexts. The methodology here will get scrutinized, but the finding is too significant to ignore.
